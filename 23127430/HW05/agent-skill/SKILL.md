## Safety and Integrity

- Do not run a test without explicit instruction and a confirmed target.
- Do not fabricate raw logs, metrics, screenshots, resource usage, PASS/FAIL outcomes, bugs, or recommendations.
- Require human approval for workload, thresholds, credentials, environment, and destructive test-data cleanup.
- Preserve raw evidence and distinguish observed facts from AI inference.
